# CROWDPLAY UX audit (judge + product-design pass)

Method: read-only. Playwright against the mock-mode server on :3200. Judge solo on `/` then `/try` (390x844 and 1440x900, 3 full rounds, 2 runs each). Hosted show: host 1440x900, `/screen` 1920x1080, 2 phones 390x844, 2 full 3-round runs. Also `/me`, `/story`, `/impact`, `/how-it-works`, 360x640 phone, keyboard-open 390x470.
Shots: `SH = H:\ClaudeData\Temp-claude\ux-audit\shots\` (names below are relative to SH, `.png`). Scripts: `H:\ClaudeData\Temp-claude\ux-audit\*.js`.
Not testable here: real LLM latency. The DISTILL view exists but mock distill resolves in about 0.5 s, so I never saw it. Real-mode narration and option text are unseen.

## Measured judge path (`/try`, mobile and desktop agree)
- Landing to first typed character: 3 taps (Try, Start the demo, focus box) and about 8 s after Start. The 6 s "Get ready" locks the composer.
- Whole demo: 153 to 154 s from Start to finale. The landing card says "(60 s)" and the intro says "about three minutes".
- Each round is about 50 s: Get ready 6, Storm 20, Distill under 1, Vote 12, Reveal 12. The judge acts for about 5 s of the vote and 20 s of the storm. About half of the 155 s is passive: 6 s locked, about 9 s waiting after the only human has voted, and about 8 s of static reveal after the narration is done.
- Minimum taps for the whole demo: about 11 (2 + 3 per round).
- Touch targets are all at least 44 px and there is no horizontal scroll at 360 px.

## 5 ideas that would make a judge smile (small)
1. **Idea takes flight (`/try` desktop).** When the judge sends, the bubble flies from the phone panel into the big-screen panel and lands with their name. Cause and effect in 400 ms.
2. **Canon moment gets sound and buzz.** `sound.ts` exists and the trophy overlay exists. Add a 1 s fanfare and `vibrate([30,40,30])` on win. The overlay's "Tap to skip" bar stays.
3. **Make the wildcard legible.** On the card: a small "🃏 only 4 of 61 wrote this" line with a flip-in when the options appear. That is the innovation, shown in one line.
4. **Micro-receipt after send.** "You're idea #14. 3 others said something similar." It proves the AI reads the crowd, and fills the 20 s of the storm with feedback.
5. **Line-credit postcard.** The `/story` and OG image highlight "Line 2, written by <you>" in the judge's own name, with the bot labels kept. It is the one thing a judge will screenshot.

## Keep as is (already works well)
- **Join ticket.** "Admit one", the name pre-filled, and one "I'm in" tap. Joining is under 5 s.
- **Host wizard.** One page with defaults and a live preview card. "Create show" works with zero edits in 0.7 s. The console is clear: big primary action per phase ("Start show", "End storm now", "Reveal now", "Next round"), Veto, Force win, Re-roll, Pause, +10 s, and a mirror of the big screen.
- **`/screen`.** The lobby (giant QR plus code tiles plus premise), the vote layout (huge type, "Leading" tag), the reveal composition and the "Canon by @names" credit line.
- **Phone play.** Live idea chat, composer chips, the vote cards with "Your vote" and "Yours" chips, haptics on vote, the "Your idea became canon" overlay, the hosted finale ("Your night" stats, "you" tags).
- **Honesty.** The bot banner, a 🤖 on every bot, "Bots and demo rooms excluded" on `/impact`, the "boosted in demo" disclosure, "Limits and honesty" on `/how-it-works`.
- **Pages and polish.** Marquee-bulb border and code boxes, `/story` as a shareable artifact, friendly 404 and bad-code pages, no horizontal overflow at 360 px, touch targets at least 44 px, no app console errors in the flows (only the `/me` 401).
